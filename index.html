How a search runs

Five stages with a weekly update from Ryan, so you always know where the search stands.

Days 1 to 5

Brief

A detailed intake session covering the role, success measures, culture, compensation and timeline. We agree what a great hire looks like before anyone is approached.

Weeks 1 to 4

Research and approach

We map the market and contact candidates directly, including leaders who are not looking and would never answer an advertisement.

Weeks 3 to 6

Assessment

Long-form interviews and referencing narrow the field to a short list, each with a written profile covering strengths, risks and motivation.

Weeks 5 to 9

Presentation and interviews

You meet the finalists. We coordinate schedules, gather feedback from both sides and keep expectations aligned.

Weeks 8 to 14, then the first year

Offer and onboarding

We negotiate the package, manage notice periods and counter-offers, and check in with you and your new leader through the first twelve months.

Questions we hear often

How are your fees structured?

Retained searches are billed in stages as a percentage of first-year cash compensation. Contingent searches are billed only when a candidate is placed. Terms are confirmed in writing before work begins.

How long does a search take?

Most executive searches deliver a first short list within four to six weeks. An appointment is typically made within eight to fourteen weeks, depending on notice periods.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. If a placed candidate leaves within the agreed guarantee period, we repeat the search without an additional professional fee. The terms are in every engagement letter.

Can a search be confidential?

Yes. We regularly handle replacements and new roles where the client name and the vacancy must stay private until the right moment.

Do you recruit across borders?

Yes. We search internationally and advise on relocation, notice practices and pay norms in each market.
